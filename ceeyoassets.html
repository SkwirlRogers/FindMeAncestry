Assettie • CeeYoAssets

Unclaimed assets are more than money in a bank.

Potential assets can sit with government agencies, former employers, pension plans, insurers, courts, financial institutions, utilities and local authorities. CeeYoAssets organizes the research paths from international and national sources down to state, county, municipal and private holders.

Current service status: This page is an official-source directory and research guide—not a live universal asset search. No centralized all-assets database is connected, no claim is being accepted or filed, and no ownership or eligibility determination has been made. Do not submit Social Security numbers, bank details, policy numbers, identity documents or claim records here.

Pensions & Retirement

Private pensions, defined-benefit plans, 401(k)s, union plans, abandoned plans, annuities and missing retirement distributions.

Airline-worker lead: the program name you may be remembering is PBGC's Pension Search Directory / Missing Participants Program. PBGC's historical outreach specifically mentioned airline workers, and its current pension-search partner list includes the Airline Pilots Association (ALPA) and Association of Flight Attendants (AFA). Those are search partners, not separate guaranteed compensation programs. Start with PBGC and DOL; other airline compensation may require the former employer, union or plan administrator.

Insurance & Annuities

Life-insurance death benefits, group policies, annuities, matured policies, unpaid claim checks, policy dividends and premium refunds.

The NAIC locator is for life-insurance policies and annuity contracts of deceased persons. Auto and property-insurance issues generally require the insurer or state insurance department.

Property Tax Refunds & Local Surplus

Duplicate tax payments, property-tax overpayments, tax-sale surplus and local-government refunds.

These are commonly handled by county or municipal tax offices. There is no single nationwide property-tax refund search; the property location and tax year matter.

Federal and state refunds, agency-issued checks, rebates, benefit payments and other government-held funds.

Different agencies maintain different records. A general directory is a starting point, not a governmentwide search.

Employment, Wages & Workers' Compensation

Unpaid wages, payroll checks, commissions, bonuses, expense reimbursements, workers' compensation payments and uncashed employment benefits.

Check the relevant state labor agency, U.S. Department of Labor or workers' compensation agency. These are separate systems from pension searches.

Financial & Tangible Property

Bank accounts, certificates of deposit, stocks, bonds, dividends, brokerage balances, utility deposits, customer credits, royalties, safe-deposit-box contents and other reportable property.

Start with the official unclaimed-property program for each relevant state, then check the named institution or holder where appropriate.

Courts, Bankruptcy & Settlements

Bankruptcy-court funds, court registry balances, settlement distributions, trust or escrow balances and other legal distributions.

A possible match is not proof of ownership or heirship. Follow the court or administrator's own identity and claim process.

Global to local

How a source-led search is organized

1. Jurisdiction

International, national/federal, state/province/territory, county/municipal, court, employer, insurer, plan administrator or private holder.

2. Asset category

Identify the type of asset and the holder most likely to maintain the record. A pension search is not an insurance search.

3. Evidence & official claim

Separate a source lead from a possible match, evidence review, identity/authority checks and the agency's final decision.

Fees and legal compliance: CeeYoAssets is not accepting claims or charging recovery-contingent fees at this time. Property-finder rules vary by jurisdiction. North Carolina has specific registration and licensing requirements and caps covered finder agreements at $1,000 or 20% of recovered value, whichever is less. Do not rely on a general percentage as universally lawful; obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review before offering paid claim assistance.
