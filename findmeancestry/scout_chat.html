Scout • Family History Research Assistant

Ask Scout

Scout helps you plan genealogy research, understand DNA-match evidence, evaluate historical records and organize next steps.

Scout provides research guidance. It does not search private member databases, log into Ancestry or MyHeritage, or access Kayla's private archive. FamilySearch Beta API authentication is not yet connected.

Scout: Tell me the family-history question you're working on. I can help organize evidence, compare possible relationships and identify appropriate official records to consult.

Privacy: Do not send Social Security numbers, passwords, raw DNA files, kit IDs, identity documents, bank details or private information about other people.
