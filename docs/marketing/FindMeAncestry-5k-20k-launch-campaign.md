**Primary conversion:** Start a useful Scout demo / begin a family-history research workflow.

**Reach objective:** 5,000–20,000 unique people reached during the launch campaign. 1,000 is the minimum checkpoint, not the campaign ceiling.

## 1. Launch positioning

**Core promise:** Make the next step in your family-history research clearer. Bring names, places, dates, records, and DNA-match questions into one guided research workspace.

**Hero line:** Your family story has clues. Let's connect them.

**Supporting copy:** FindMeAncestry brings genealogy research tools, historical-record discovery workflows, DNA-match learning, and Scout's guided research experience together. Start with the details you know; build a better-supported research path from there.

**Primary CTA:** Try Scout's demo.  
**Secondary CTA:** Explore research tools.  
**Separate asset CTA:** Have a question about unclaimed property? Meet Kayla and continue to CeeYoAssets.

### Claims control

Do not publish claims that the platform searches billions of records, queries twelve live partner databases, guarantees a match, provides unlimited live research, or has a verified five-star SEO rating unless each claim is independently tested and evidenced. Describe Scout as an AI-assisted genealogy research guide, not a licensed or credentialed human investigator. Explain that records and DNA interpretations require source review and are not guaranteed.

## 2. Audience segments

1. **Family-history beginners:** people who have names and family stories but do not know which record to search next.
2. **Genealogy brick-wall researchers:** experienced hobbyists tracing a line that has stalled.
3. **DNA-match learners:** people trying to understand shared centimorgans, match clusters, and triangulation concepts.
4. **Family historians and reunion organizers:** people organizing evidence, timelines, surnames, and migration history.
5. **Adoptees and biological-family researchers:** people exploring records and research options; do not imply that the software itself guarantees identification or reunification.

Do not upload client case lists, family trees, DNA files, or private research records to advertising platforms or general marketing audiences.

## 7. Search engine strategy

### Priority topic clusters
- How to start genealogy research with limited information
- How to search historical census and vital records
- How to research ancestors when county boundaries changed
- How to interpret shared centimorgans in genealogy
- What DNA triangulation means—and what it cannot prove
- How to organize surname variants and migration timelines
- How to evaluate online family-tree hints and source citations

### On-page requirements
- One unique title and meta description per indexable page.
- One descriptive H1 per page; logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
- Canonical URL only after the production hostname is confirmed.
- XML sitemap listing only canonical, public, indexable, non-duplicate pages.
- robots.txt references the sitemap and disallows private/API routes.
- Open Graph and social-card metadata with a real approved brand image.
- Descriptive internal links; avoid orphan pages and broken links.
- Breadcrumbs and relevant structured data only where visible page content supports it.
- Image alt text describes the image; it does not stuff keywords.
- Mobile performance, accessibility, HTTPS, and real contact/privacy/terms links.
- Remove placeholder links and fix malformed HTML before release.

### Measurement
- Connect the canonical site to Google Search Console and GA4 only after access is authorized.
- Track organic clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, indexed pages, branded vs non-branded queries, engaged sessions, demo starts, and qualified enquiries.
- Do not describe an SEO score as “five-star” unless a named audit tool has actually scored the exact production site. Aim for passing technical checks and improving real search performance.

## 8. Commercial-readiness release gates

**Do not launch paid conversion traffic until all gates pass:**

1. **Pricing:** public schedule is canonical: $9.99 / $19.99 / $29.99 / $49.99 monthly. Update all plan tables and server-side plan records consistently.
2. **Payments:** replace the simulated Google Pay response with a real payment processor integration; verify successful/failed payment, webhook signature, recurring renewal, cancellation, refund handling, tax disclosures, and customer receipt.

5. **Privacy:** isolate FindMeAncestry family/DNA uploads from the shared NUTTEROS library. Do not migrate or inspect customer payloads as part of marketing.
6. **SEO:** fix HTML validation errors, create and validate sitemap, confirm canonical host, verify robots rules, add metadata, and register Search Console.
7. **Analytics:** implement consent-aware analytics and conversion events before campaign launch.
8. **Support and legal:** publish real privacy, terms, refund/cancellation, contact, and support information; test the contact route.
9. **QA:** mobile, desktop, keyboard, accessibility, links, forms, checkout, and end-to-end synthetic user journey.
10. **Deployment:** build from a clean, reproducible commit; deploy to staging first; verify the production artifact and rollback.
