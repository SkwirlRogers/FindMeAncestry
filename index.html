Glossary of Common Genealogy & DNA Terms

Extracted from Unraveling Your Roots. Free terms available to all; full comprehensive dictionary unlocked with subscription.

Centimorgan (cM)

A unit of measurement for genetic linkage, used to express the distance between chromosome positions and calculate relationship proximity.

GEDCOM File

GEnealogical Data COMmunication file format used to exchange family tree data between different genealogy software programs.

Chromosome Triangulation

The process of identifying three or more people who share a common segment of DNA on the same chromosome inherited from a shared ancestor.

Habitation Theoretics

Reconstructing historical family movement and residence timelines based on land deeds, tax registers, and census schedules.
