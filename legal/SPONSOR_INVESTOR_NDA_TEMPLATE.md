# Sponsor and Investor NDA Template

This Confidential Information and Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of [Date] between [Company Name] ("Disclosing Party") and [Recipient Name / Entity] ("Recipient").

1. Purpose
Recipient may receive confidential and proprietary information from Disclosing Party regarding the Project, business, technology, product, roadmap, architecture, financial information, user metrics, and related materials (the "Confidential Information").

2. Definition of Confidential Information
Confidential Information includes, without limitation, all non-public information disclosed orally, visually, electronically, in writing, or otherwise, including but not limited to:
- product strategy and roadmap
- code, architecture, and technical specifications
- financial and operational information
- customer and user data
- marketing plans and sales information
- business models, pricing, and strategy
- brand, trademarks, and product identity
- technical designs, schemas, and workflows
- any notes, drafts, screenshots, demos, or materials related to the Project

3. Protection
Recipient shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose, copy, reproduce, summarize, or distribute it to any third party without the prior written consent of Disclosing Party.

4. Use Limitation
Recipient may use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship, investment, sponsorship, partnership, or collaboration with Disclosing Party.

5. No Reverse Engineering or Extraction
Recipient shall not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, inspect, or otherwise attempt to extract proprietary information from any software, materials, or systems belonging to Disclosing Party.

6. Return or Destruction
Upon written request from Disclosing Party, Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all copies of Confidential Information in Recipient’s possession or control, except for archival copies retained solely to comply with legal obligations.

7. Ownership
All Confidential Information remains the sole and exclusive property of Disclosing Party. No license, right, or ownership interest is granted to Recipient under this Agreement.

8. No Public Statements
Recipient shall not publicly disclose any information about the Project, its business, or the relationship without written consent from Disclosing Party.

9. Term
This Agreement shall remain in effect for [three (3)] years from the date of disclosure, or for as long as the Confidential Information remains confidential under applicable law, whichever is longer.

10. Remedies
Recipient acknowledges that any unauthorized disclosure of Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm to Disclosing Party, and that monetary damages may be inadequate. Disclosing Party shall be entitled to injunctive relief and any other remedies available at law or equity.

11. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state or jurisdiction in which Disclosing Party is located.
