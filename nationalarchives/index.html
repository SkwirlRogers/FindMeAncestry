Core Research Record Types

📊

Census Records

Federal census 1790–1950, state censuses, mortality schedules, agricultural & manufacturing schedules.

⚖

Military Records

Service records, pension files, draft registrations, bounty land warrants, veteran cemetery records.

✈

Immigration Records

Ship manifests, naturalization records, passenger lists, border crossing records, alien registration.

📋

Vital Records

Birth, marriage, and death certificates. Divorce records. State vital records office directories.

🏠

Land Records

Deeds, grants, patents, homestead records, Bureau of Land Management tract books, plats.

⚖

Court Records

Civil & criminal case files, equity dockets, court journals, circuit court records.

📜

Probate Records

Wills, estate inventories, administration bonds, guardianship records, probate packets.

⛪

Church Records

Baptismal registers, membership rolls, marriage records, burial records, church histories.
